Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our productized models.

Why productized packages instead of hourly rates?

Hourly billing incentivizes slow work. Productized tiers focus entirely on value, giving you a fixed cost and guaranteed delivery. You know exactly what you are paying and what business outcomes to expect.

What is included in the Dynamic Web Platform?

Our dynamic platforms are engineered for rapid scaling and high conversion. You get a fully customized, ultra-fast website paired with a built-in CRM for real-time lead capture, an easy-to-use CMS for seamless content updates, automated SEO architecture, and 100/100 performance scores out of the box.

What is included in the Content Marketing Retainer?

Our growth retainer goes beyond simple posting. It includes a comprehensive initial strategy document, an iterative weekly improvement plan, programmatic SEO target architectures, and AEO/GEO (Answer Engine / Generative Engine Optimization) content frameworks to ensure search engines choose your brand as the definitive answer.

How does managed hosting & security work?

High-speed, enterprise-grade hosting is included free for all clients on our monthly marketing retainers. For one-time build projects (Static Landing Page or Dynamic Web Platform), managed hosting ranges from $99 to $199 per month depending on traffic and bandwidth usage. This covers global CDN distribution, DDoS protection, automated SSL certificates, and 99.9% uptime monitoring.

Is there a contract or can we cancel?

All monthly content marketing retainers are billed month-to-month. You can cancel or pause at any time with a 15-day notice. One-time setup projects require a 50% deposit upfront and 50% upon completion.
