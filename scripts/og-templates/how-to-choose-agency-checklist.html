BEFORE YOU SIGN

How to choose the right digital marketing agency

Five questions that separate a real partner from a retainer.
1
Who actually does the work?
Many agencies sell you the founder, then hand you a junior. Ask what’s subcontracted.
BAIT & SWITCH
2
What does it cost — before the proposal call?
Two meetings before a number usually means the price is based on what you’ll pay.
PRICE OPACITY
3
How long is the contract?
A 12-month lock-in removes the incentive to show results by month three.
LOCK-IN
4
How fast is their own website?
Run it through PageSpeed Insights. A slow SEO agency is telling you something.
PROOF
5
What exactly will they measure?
Leads and booked jobs. Impressions and rankings alone don’t pay the retainer.
VANITY METRICS
