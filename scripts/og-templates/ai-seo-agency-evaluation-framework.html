How to evaluate an AI SEO agency

Five litmus tests that separate real GEO practitioners from rebranded retainers.
1
Can they show live, verified AI citations?
Ask to see live client citations in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews — not hypothetical slides.
CITATION PROOF
2
Do they possess true technical & programmatic SEO depth?
AI engines cite clean entities, schema graphs, and fast edge architecture — not thin auto-generated spam.
TECHNICAL DEPTH
3
Is pricing productized and upfront before the sales call?
Agencies that hide rates until discovery calls usually price according to your perceived bank balance.
PRICE TRANSPARENCY
4
Who actually architects and executes the work?
Ask if senior technical engineers build your system, or if your account is handed to offshore prompt jockeys.
WHO DOES WORK
5
What is the contract term and accountability structure?
A 12-month lock-in shields poor execution. Performance accountability thrives under agile, month-to-month terms.
NO LOCK-IN TRAPS
