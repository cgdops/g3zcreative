# 05 — Dental AI Slot Outbound & Sales CRM Playbook

Internal SOP for acquiring solo dental practices using the **Google AI Mode Deduplication** wedge and closing **The Local AI Monopoly System** (`/search-to-seat-system.html`).

> **Interactive CRM Version:** Open `/crm-dental-playbook.html` (or click **🦷 Dental Playbook** in the CRM header) for live merge fields, 1-click copy buttons, and a 1-click personalized Zoom Stack Page launcher.

---

## Core Mechanism (Why This Converts)

Google AI Mode does not rank "the top 3 general dentists"—it curates **3 non-overlapping specialty slots** so it doesn't show three identical offices. Solo practices with `4.8★–5.0★` and `150–580+ reviews` are routinely **excluded** simply for overlapping with a neighbor or sharing a plaza.

---

## Stage 0: Target Selection (5 Minutes / Zip Code)

Run these two prompts in Google AI Mode:
1. `solo dental practices near [City]`
2. `Why did you choose to show me these? What other solo dental practices are near [City] that aren't shown here, including why each one wasn't shown?`

**Qualifying Criteria:**
- Independent / solo dentist (not a corporate DSO).
- `4.7★–5.0★` rating with `150+` Google reviews.
- Explicitly excluded from the initial 3-pack due to overlap/proximity, while Google AI notes an under-leveraged differentiator (e.g., sedation, LVI cosmetic, in-house uninsured plan, late/Saturday hours, spa-like transparent pricing).

## Stage 1: 60-Second Loom Script

Record while scrolling directly to the **"Why it wasn't shown"** bullet under their practice:

- **0:00–0:10 (Hook):** *"Hey Dr. [Name], I ran a quick Google AI Mode search for solo dentists in [City] today and noticed something weird about [Practice Name]."*
- **0:10–0:35 (Proof):** *"You have [Stars & Reviews], which is incredible—but Google AI explicitly left you off the top 3 shortlist because it thinks you overlap with [Picked Neighbor] down the road."*
- **0:35–0:50 (Open Slot):** *"The crazy part is Google AI already noticed your real edge—[Open Specialty Slot]—it just isn't connecting that to the main search slot yet."*
- **0:50–1:00 (CTA):** *"I mapped out the 3 changes to claim that uncontested slot in [City]. Worth a quick 15-minute walkthrough between patients this week? No pitch—I'll just show you the exact map."*

## Stage 3: 15-Minute "AI Slot Audit" Call Flow

Before joining Zoom, open `/search-to-seat-system.html` pre-filled with their URL params (via the CRM Playbook launcher).

1. **0:00–3:00 (Live Proof):** Show Google AI Mode live. Ask: *"If a patient with a \$5,000 case searches this tonight, who gets the call?"*
2. **3:00–6:00 (Anchor ROI):** Ask their average Year-1 high-margin case value. Plug it into the live ROI slider on `/search-to-seat-system.html` at a conservative `2 patients/month`.
3. **6:00–11:00 (Present the Stack):** Walk through the **\$8,500 Local AI Monopoly Stack**:
   - Core Engine: Uncontested AI Specialty Overhaul (\$4,500 value)
   - Bonus #1: Signal-Rich Review Accelerator (\$1,500 value)
   - Bonus #2: AI-Ready Conversion Kit + 1-Page Front Desk Script (\$1,000 value)
   - Bonus #3: Monthly AI Shortlist Defense & Call Attribution (\$1,500 value)
4. **11:00–15:00 (Scarcity, Guarantee & Close):**
   - **Option A:** \$3,000 one-time 60-day build + \$1,250/mo starting Month 3 (month-to-month).
   - **Option B:** \$1,750/mo 90-day pilot (\$5,250 total), then \$1,250/mo month-to-month.
   - **Guarantee:** Appear in Google AI Mode's shortlist for target specialty prompts in 60 days, or we work for free until you do.
   - **Scarcity:** 1 practice per specialty per 5-mile radius.

## Stage 4 & 5: On-Call Close & First 72 Hours

- **On Zoom:** Collect payment via Stripe + 1-page agreement before hanging up.
- **Hour 1:** Send intake form (GBP manager invite, website CMS access, top 1–2 high-margin procedures). Mark zip + specialty **LOCKED** in CRM.
- **Day 2:** 30-minute kickoff call with doctor + office manager; deliver 1-page Front Desk script.
- **Day 7 (Fast Win):** Ship GBP overhaul, first specialty hub page + JSON-LD entity schema, and SMS review trigger.
